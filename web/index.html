    How accurate is it?

    A probability can't be judged on one day: a 30% chance that doesn't burn wasn't wrong. Accuracy shows over many forecasts, measured three ways: are the riskiest cells really where fires happen, do the percentages match how often fires follow, and is it better than the obvious alternative of assuming today's fires continue.

    About

    Titik api is Indonesian for hotspot. Every evening at 19:00 WIB, TitikApi takes the fires seen by NASA's NOAA-20 satellite and the latest ECMWF weather, and forecasts the chance of a fire in every 11 km cell of Sumatra and Kalimantan for each of the next 7 days. The forecast combines a deep-learning model (a U-Net, which reads the whole map at once) with a gradient-boosted model, both trained on fires from 2012 to 2020.

    Recent fires and each place's usual fire season carry most of the forecast. A week ahead, the deep-learning model leans on how long it has been since rain, which is why it holds up better than simpler models at longer range.

    This is a portfolio project, not an official warning. For official hotspot information, see SiPongi, Indonesia's national forest and land fire monitoring system, and BMKG.
